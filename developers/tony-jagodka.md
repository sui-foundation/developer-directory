## Experience Statement

I'm a solo founder (UIG Studios LLC) who designs and ships complete, end-to-end products on Sui — Move contracts, backends, and frontends.

My flagship is **Proof of Real (PoR)**, a Sui-native proof-of-personhood credential: a soulbound on-chain credential proving a real human performed a real-world action, with no biometrics and no PII. It's deployed to testnet and mainnet, has an npm-published SDK (`por-sdk`, Apache-2.0) and on-chain `require_verified` gating, and includes Sui-native Groth16 (BN254) verification of an external uniqueness nullifier plus zkLogin + Enoki sponsored-transaction onboarding (no seed phrase, no gas, no wallet).

I also built the **first x402 payment facilitator to settle on Sui** — mainnet-live, real USDC settled, non-custodial, zero-fee, with an Enoki gas station for sponsored agent payments (open-source, Apache-2.0; submitted to the x402 facilitators directory). On top of these I shipped **Acre** ("Walk Your City"), a fully gasless presence game that Sybil-gates its check-ins on PoR credentials, and **FairLine**, a DeepBook CLOB vault built for Sui Overflow.

My day-to-day spans Move, PTBs, zkLogin, Groth16/ZK verification, DeepBook, Enoki sponsorship, and agentic (x402) payments. Everything above was designed, built, and deployed solo.
